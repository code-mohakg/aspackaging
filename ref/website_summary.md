## 1. Company Overview
Customised Packaging is one of the **largest independent corrugated manufacturing units** in Mumbai, based in Goregaon East. They position themselves as a "one-stop-shop" for all packaging solutions, emphasizing precision craftsmanship and personalized service.

### Key Value Propositions
*   **Customization:** Bespoke designs, specific dimensions, and advanced printing (internal/external).
*   **Sustainability:** Integration of eco-friendly practices and recyclable materials.
*   **Expertise:** Professionals dedicated to creating "ideal aesthetics" for branded boxes.
*   **Reliability:** Focus on competitive pricing and timely delivery for long-term relationships.

## 4. Detailed Product Catalog

### B. Bakery & Sweet Packaging
*   **Cake Packaging:**
    *   **Window Boxes:** Tall cake boxes with clear windows to showcase designs.
    *   **Printed Options:** Vibrant colors, intricate patterns, and personalized branding.
    *   **Variety:** Plain white boxes for simplicity/sophistication; premium heavy-duty cake bases.
*   **Confectionery (Mithai) & Chocolate:**
    *   **Cavity Boxes:** Multi-purpose cavity boxes (4, 6, and custom counts).
    *   **Design Styles:** Traditional Mithai boxes, Foldable sweet boxes, and Laminated fancy boxes.
    *   **Safety:** Uses food-grade materials to maintain taste and freshness.

### C. Specialized Food & Retail Packaging
*   **Fruit Packaging:**
    *   **Mango Boxes:** Ventilated and sturdy corrugated designs.
    *   **Pomegranate Boxes:** Premium printed boxes designed for market visibility.
    *   **Dry Fruit Boxes:** Elegant gift-style packaging.
*   **Fast Food:** 
    *   **Burger & Pizza Boxes:** Designed to retain heat and prevent moisture buildup.
*   **Other Sectors:** Cosmetic & Pharmaceutical boxes, Garment packaging, and Electronic product boxes.
